# Service Statement

The questions that drive my research, about access, opportunity and who gets to participate fully in education, also shape how I serve. Throughout my academic career, I have served the communities I belong to as a student leader, a mentor, a peer reviewer and an organizer of scholarly exchange. For me, service is where commitments to equity and opportunity become everyday practice: building institutions and communities in which everyone has a fair chance to contribute and thrive.

> Service is where commitments to equity and opportunity become everyday practice.

## Student leadership and governance

At The University of Alabama, I was elected Speaker of the House of Delegates of the Graduate Student Association (2024–2025) and then Executive Vice President (2025–2026). In these roles, I presided over legislative deliberations using Robert's Rules of Order, oversaw the onboarding and training of delegates, and served as the link between the House of Delegates and the Executive Council, supporting graduate student advocacy and campus governance.

At Beijing Normal University, I served the international students of the Faculty of Education first as Minister of Academics (2017–2018) and then as President of the International Students Association (2018–2019). I advocated for translating key digital library resources from Chinese to English, and I conceived and launched the faculty's first International Student–Faculty Conference, a space where students and faculty could exchange research as colleagues.

## Community engagement and mentoring

As Service Chair of the African Students Association at Alabama (2024–2025), I coordinated the association's annual "Helping Hands" initiative, organized inclusive recreation activities at the Tuscaloosa Tennis Center in partnership with Eagle Wings Kids and United Cerebral Palsy of West Alabama, and led the association's participation in the 2025 Martin Luther King Jr. Day of Service.

Mentoring has been a constant thread in my service. As Executive Director and board member of Global Academic Bridge, I help expand equitable access to international education, scholarships and career pathways. Through the Deeper Life Campus Fellowship, I have mentored students and young adults at the University of Ibadan, the University of Ikere and The University of Alabama in their academic development, leadership growth and career direction.

## Service to the profession

I contribute to the quality of scholarship in my field as a peer reviewer for the *International Journal of Manpower* (Emerald), *Discover Education* (Springer Nature), the *African Journal of Economic and Sustainable Development* (Inderscience), *Economic Alternatives* and the *International E-Journal of Advances in Social Sciences*. I also review conference proposals for the Comparative and International Education Society and the Council for the Study of Community Colleges.

As a member of the International Organizing Committee of the International Strategic Research Congress, I help plan international conferences that connect scholars across Europe, Asia and Africa. I am an active member of the American Educational Research Association, the Association for the Study of Higher Education, the National Education Finance Academy, the Comparative and International Education Society, the Comparative Education Society of Asia and the Council for the Study of Community Colleges.

## Looking ahead

Whether I am chairing a meeting, reviewing a manuscript or mentoring a student, my aim is the same: to strengthen the communities that make scholarship and learning possible, and to help ensure that more people can take part in them.
